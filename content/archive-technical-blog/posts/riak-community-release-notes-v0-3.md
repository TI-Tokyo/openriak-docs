*June 4, 2012*

I’m thrilled to announce that the [v0.3 Riak Community Release Notes](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.3.md) are now official. (For some history on the Community Release Notes, go [here](./riak-community-release-notes.md).) This installment covers what happened in the community from (approximately) May 4 through June 1. Some of the many stand-out accomplishments from this release:

- The team at Kiip [gave a great talk](http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/05/25/Scaling-Riak-At-Kiip/) on moving from MongoDB to Riak.
- Long-time Riak and Riak Search users and contributors Cliboard [officially launched](http://blog.clipboard.com/2012/05/31/0-Going-Public).
- The [first Riak London Meetup](http://www.meetup.com/riak-london/events/62061262/) was held.
- Mathias Meyer [shipped a huge update](http://www.paperplanes.de/2012/5/30/riak-handbook-11-is-out.html) to the Riak Handbook.
- Matt Ranney and the team at Voxer [released their Node.js client](https://github.com/mranney/node_riak) for Riak.

We did a lot more. [Take a few minutes to read up](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.3.md). Also, we’re already rolling with the [0.4 Release Notes](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.4.md) (which will cover June 2 up through July 1). You’re encouraged to contribute to past, present, and future release notes, so don’t hold back.

Enjoy, and thanks for being a part of Riak.
