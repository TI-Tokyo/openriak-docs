*May 9, 2012*

Today I am excited to introduce a new piece of infrastructure to the Riak Community on which we’ve been working: [Riak Community Release Notes](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community).

Much like codebases grow and evolve, so does a community and its accomplishments. Why not present and chronicle the community in the same way you would a piece of code? The Riak Community Release Notes are an attempt to do just that.

Each month, we’ll tag and release a new “version” of the Riak Community. **The most recent (and first official) release is [v0.2](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md)**. Each release will represent the evolution of the Community as demonstrated by our collective work and activity. For example:

- There were [more than 20 blog posts](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md#blog-posts-and-podcasts) about Riak in April
- Some of our users [raised funding and welcomed new children to their family](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md#other-awesome-things-riak-users-did)
- We released [some](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md#documentation) (but not enough) new documentation.

My hope is that this will grow into a [collaborative effort](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community#contributing) to track the trajectory of Riak and our user community. It looks somewhat like the [Riak Recap](http://recap.basho.com/2012/05/04/riak-recap-for-april-27-may-4/), but I think it’ll extend and surpass it in a lot of ways. Most importantly, it’s an experiment, and I’m looking forward to how it evolves. Pull requests, feedback, and criticisms are welcomed.

Thanks for being a part of Riak.
