*August 14, 2013*

Next week, we will be hosting the “What’s New in Riak CS 1.4” webcast. Join us on Friday, August 23rd at 11am PT/2pm ET for a free webcast that will discuss the new features and updates announced with the latest release. You can sign up for this 30-minute webcast [here](http://info.basho.com/RiakCS1.4_Aug23.html).

In addition to looking at the 1.4 updates, this webcast will discuss the basics of Riak CS and Riak CS Enterprise, while also providing some common use cases and user stories.

[Register now](http://info.basho.com/RiakCS1.4_Aug23.html) for the “What’s New in Riak CS 1.4” webcast and learn more about this new release [here](http://basho.com/riak-cs-1-4-is-now-available/).
