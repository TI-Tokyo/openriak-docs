*July 28, 2010*

Sometimes you need more than words to illustrate a point. Here is Basho’s humble attempt to clarify the difference between “Dynamo-Style” systems (like Riak) that use [consistent hashing](http://wiki.basho.com/Riak-Glossary.html#Consistent-Hashing) to achieve fault tolerance, simple scaling, and prevent data loss, and systems that use techniques like [sharding](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharding).
