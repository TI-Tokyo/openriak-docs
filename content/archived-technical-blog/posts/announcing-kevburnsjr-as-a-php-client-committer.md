*February 28, 2011*

We just added Kevin Burns, who goes by [KevBurnsJr on Github](https://github.com/KevBurnsJr), [Twitter](http://twitter.com/kevburnsjr) and the #riak IRC room on irc.freenode.net, as a committer to the Basho-supported Riak PHP Client.

Kevin has been hard at work over the past few weeks adding some great functionality to the PHP client and has even kicked off porting [Ripple](https://github.com/basho/ripple), Basho’s Ruby Client ODM, to PHP. Suffice it to say that we at Basho are excited about Kevin’s participation and involvement with Riak and our PHP code.

Some relevant code:

\* [Riak’s PHP Client](https://github.com/basho/riak-php-client)  
\* [Port of Ripple to PHP](https://github.com/KevBurnsJr/ripple-php)

Thank, Kev! We are looking forward to your contributions.
