*July 6, 2012*

I’m excited to announce that [v0.4 of the Riak Community Release Notes](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.4.md) are official, covering what happened in the Riak Community from approximately June 1 thru June 30. Some highlights include:

- Matt Ranney and Ryan Sokol discussed [Riak in Production at Voxer](http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/06/27/Riak-at-Voxer/).
- Sean Cribbs gave [a talk on CRDT’s](https://vimeo.com/43903960) at Berlin Buzzwords.
- The [inaugural Paris Riak Meetup](http://www.meetup.com/Paris-Riak-Meetup/events/64079822/) was held.
- The team at Near Infinity [wrote a good introduction to Riak](http://www.nearinfinity.com/blogs/andrew_crute/2012/06/18/a-brief-introduction-to-riak.html).

The community also shipped a bunch of code during the month of June, so if you have a few minutes [read what else the Riak Community accomplished](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.4.md). Also, we’re already rolling with the [0.5 Release Notes](https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.5.md) (which will cover July 2 up through August 1). You’re encouraged to contribute to past, present, and future release notes, so don’t hold back.

Enjoy, and thanks for being a part of Riak.
