[Booking.com](http://booking.com/) manages billions of events every day. However, these events are schema-less and therefore a difficult task for standard analytics tools to handle. That’s why [Booking.com](http://booking.com/) has implemented a solution built on Riak KV. They’ve built an Events Storage Database to address near-real-time analysis of the data flow. It’s allowed [Booking.com](http://booking.com/) to push the boundaries of innovation and keep monitoring and analysing the whole platform while managing hundreds of thousands of properties.

This post, cowritten by Damien Krotkine and Ivan Paponov, shares how [Booking.com](http://booking.com/) uses Riak to constantly monitor, inspect and analyze its platform. Part three of a four part series is now live and describing exactly how and why Riak gets used at [Booking.com](http://booking.com/).
