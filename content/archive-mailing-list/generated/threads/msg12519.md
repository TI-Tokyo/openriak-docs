There aren&#x27;t any docs for that particular case. In fact, this is yet
another example of why we no longer recommend using Links at all.

If you&#x27;re just beginning to design a production Riak model I&#x27;d recommend
investigating alternative models that can leverage direct key lookups, or
2i, or yokozuna.
