A content type is a cue as to what kind of data you&#x27;re storing in Riak. The
concept is based on Internet media types [1]. The cue may or may not be
important to your application to figure out what to do with the data. It&#x27;s also
important for certain Riak features like Riak Search, where you can
automatically index data based on it&#x27;s content type (XML, JSON, etc.). If all
you&#x27;re storing is plain text, you might as well ignore it and store everything
as application/octet-stream, but on the other hand, it doesn&#x27;t hurt to just
specify text/plain and have it properly marked as what kind of data it is.
