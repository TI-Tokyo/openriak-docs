Riak Recap for June 15 - 19
=====================

1) Francisco Treacy (@frank06) and the Riak-js crew tagged the 0.4.0
release today (and reached a bit of a milestone on GitHub).

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="https://twitter.com/#!/riakjs/status/82546215908155392">https://twitter.com/#!/riakjs/status/82546215908155392</a>

Congrats!

2) It would appear that Francisco had a busy weekend: he also released
a small query language for Riak that uses JSONSelect. By his own
admission it&#x27;s not intended for production use, but it&#x27;s certainly
worth taking a look at.

Code on GitHub here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/frank06/riak-ql">https://github.com/frank06/riak-ql</a>

Thanks, Francisco!

3) Jon Brisbin updated his Riak and RabbitMQ custom exchange for the
2.5 release.

* Code here ---&gt; <a href="http://twitter.com/#!/j_brisbin/statuses/82814143488471040">http://twitter.com/#!/j_brisbin/statuses/82814143488471040</a>

4) Mark your calendars: it was just announced that Sean Cribbs
(@seancribbs) will be giving his &quot;Wrap Your SQL Head Around Riak
MapReduce&quot; talk at Strange Loop in September.

* Details here
---&gt;<a href="http://twitter.com/#!/strangeloop_stl/status/82571355542929408">http://twitter.com/#!/strangeloop_stl/status/82571355542929408</a>

4) Basho CTO Justin Sheehy (@justinsheehy) gave a short and insightful
interview to RedMonk&#x27;s @cote at Velocity last week. This one is all
about Riak, NoSQL, and Open Source.

Watch here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.redmonk.com/cote/2011/06/20/justin-sheehy-on-basho-nosql/">http://www.redmonk.com/cote/2011/06/20/justin-sheehy-on-basho-nosql/</a>

5) Ryan Zezeski (@rzezeski) just added a new entry to his Riak Core
working blog &quot;Try Try Try.&quot; This installment is an extensive look at
eventual consistency and conflict resolution.

* Grab yourself a beverage of choice and settle in for a great read
---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/rzezeski/try-try-try/tree/master/2011/riak-core-conflict-resolution">https://github.com/rzezeski/try-try-try/tree/master/2011/riak-core-conflict-resolution</a>

6) They posted the slides from Marcus Kern&#x27;s and David Dawson&#x27;s Erlang
Factory Talk. This one is called &quot;Erlang runs TV votes on Facebook&quot;
and is well worth your time. (Slides 11-12 are my personal favorites.)

* Check them out here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/404/MIGErlangFactoryLondon2011.pdf">http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/404/MIGErlangFactoryLondon2011.pdf</a>

7) fakepop contributed a small change to the Erlang bucket importer
function that&#x27;s part of Riak Function Contrib.

* Function here on RFC ---&gt; <a href="http://contrib.basho.com/bucket_importer.html">http://contrib.basho.com/bucket_importer.html</a>
* Diff here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_function_contrib/pull/11/files">https://github.com/basho/riak_function_contrib/pull/11/files</a>

Thanks, fp!

8) We added a list of the various Riak recipes, cookbooks, and
configurations to the Wiki.

* Check it out here ---&gt;
<a href="http://wiki.basho.com/Community-Developed-Libraries-and-Projects.html#Recipes%2C-Cookbooks%2C-and-Configurations">http://wiki.basho.com/Community-Developed-Libraries-and-Projects.html#Recipes%2C-Cookbooks%2C-and-Configurations</a>

If you know of any that I missed, please fork and add when you get the chance.

9) One new job to pass along.

* The awesome crew at Strobe is looking for a DevOps Engineer with
Riak experience ---&gt;
<a href="http://strobe.theresumator.com/apply/5xjbjW/DevOps-Engineer-061115.html">http://strobe.theresumator.com/apply/5xjbjW/DevOps-Engineer-061115.html</a>
