Riak Recap for February 6 - 7
======================

1) Jared sent this to the list yesterday but it&#x27;s worth including in the
Recap again: Riak 1.1RC1 was just released. There&#x27;s a lot of good stuff in
this release, and we would love some people to take it for a spin before
it&#x27;s official.

* All the details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2012-February/007462.html">http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2012-February/007462.html</a>

2) Basho is officially a sponsor of the Travis CI project. Travis is a
distributed build system that you should all take a look at.

* Travis Crowd Funding Page ---&gt; <a href="https://love.travis-ci.org/">https://love.travis-ci.org/</a>
* Short blog post about why we donated ---&gt;
<a href="http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/02/07/Basho-is-Proud-to-Sponsor-TravisCI/">http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/02/07/Basho-is-Proud-to-Sponsor-TravisCI/</a>

3) Sean Cribbs will be at the LOPSA NYC meetup on February 15 to talk about
Riak.

* Details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.lopsa-nyc.org/content/riak-distributed-replicated-fault-tolerant-key-value-database">http://www.lopsa-nyc.org/content/riak-distributed-replicated-fault-tolerant-key-value-database</a>

4) Basho is sponsoring Devopsdays Austin in April. Let me know if you&#x27;ll be
in attendance.

* Conf details here ---&gt; <a href="http://devopsdays.org/events/2012-austin/">http://devopsdays.org/events/2012-austin/</a>
