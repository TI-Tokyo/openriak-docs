Riak Recap for November 23 - 27
========================

1) Version 0.0.2 of resourceful-riak is out.

* Code and details here ---&gt;
<a href="https://twitter.com/#!/nodenpm/status/141165957975523328">https://twitter.com/#!/nodenpm/status/141165957975523328</a>

2) Basho CTO Justin Sheehy will be speaking about Riak at the Twin
Cities NoSQL Meetup next week in Richfield, MN.

* Details and registration here ---&gt;
<a href="http://twincitiesnosqlmeetup.eventbrite.com/">http://twincitiesnosqlmeetup.eventbrite.com/</a>

3) Jeremy Walworth released a Password Vault that uses Riak as the
backing store.

* Code here on GitHub ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/jeremyfsu/Riak-Backed-Password-Vault">https://github.com/jeremyfsu/Riak-Backed-Password-Vault</a>

4) Basho Team Members Steve Vinoski and Justin Sheehy collaborated
with Kresten Krab Thorup and Debasish Ghosh on a short paper called
&quot;Programming language impact on the development of distributed
system&quot;.

* Definitely worth the read ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.springerlink.com/content/w071241962270242/fulltext.pdf">http://www.springerlink.com/content/w071241962270242/fulltext.pdf</a>

5) We are doing office hours this Thursday at BashoWest in San Francisco.

* Details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/42645002/">http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/42645002/</a>
