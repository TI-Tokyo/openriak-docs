Riak Recap for January 26 - 30
=======================

1) For anyone in or around Austin, TX: they just opened up the CFP for
&quot;Devopsdays Austin&quot; happening in April. I&#x27;m sending stickers and
t-shirts to anyone who submits a Riak-related talk.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://devopsdays.org/events/2012-austin/proposals/">http://devopsdays.org/events/2012-austin/proposals/</a>

2) There is a new question up on StackOverflow about using Bitcask
that needs an answer if anyone has a moment.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://stackoverflow.com/questions/9056052/how-to-use-bitcask-as-a-stand-alone">http://stackoverflow.com/questions/9056052/how-to-use-bitcask-as-a-stand-alone</a>

3) I gave a presentation about Basho, Riak, and the Riak Community at
ScaleConf last week. Thanks to all of you for making our community
worth presenting on. :)

* Slides are here for anyone interested ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.themarkphillips.com//2012/01/27/Building-Healthy-Distributed-Systems-ScaleConf.html">http://www.themarkphillips.com//2012/01/27/Building-Healthy-Distributed-Systems-ScaleConf.html</a>

4) Basho Dev Andrew Thompson wrote about blog post about QuickChecking
Poolboy. (Poolboy is an Erlang worker pool that was an addition to
Riak in the 1.0 release.)

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/01/27/Quick-Checking-Poolboy-for-Fun-and-Profit/">http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/01/27/Quick-Checking-Poolboy-for-Fun-and-Profit/</a>
