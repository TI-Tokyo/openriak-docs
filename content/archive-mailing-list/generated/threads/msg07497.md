Riak Recap for May 14 - 20
====================

1) Matt Ranney and the team at Voxer released their production Node.js
client for Riak.

* Details and code here on the Basho Blog ---&gt;
<a href="http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/05/14/Voxers-NodeJS-Client-Now-Open-Source/">http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/05/14/Voxers-NodeJS-Client-Now-Open-Source/</a>

2) David Dawson and Marcus Kern gave a talk in London a few weeks back
called &quot;Real Life Riak at MIG&quot;.

* Watch here ---&gt;
<a href="http://skillsmatter.com/podcast/nosql/mobile-interactive-group-riak">http://skillsmatter.com/podcast/nosql/mobile-interactive-group-riak</a>

3) Ian Plosker gave a &quot;Introduction to Riak&quot; talk at that same event.

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://skillsmatter.com/podcast/nosql/denormalised-riak">http://skillsmatter.com/podcast/nosql/denormalised-riak</a>

4) Tim Douglas and the team at Bump wrote a great blog post on how and why
they made the transition to Riak from MongoDB.

* Read here ---&gt; <a href="http://devblog.bu.mp/from-mongodb-to-riak-7138">http://devblog.bu.mp/from-mongodb-to-riak-7138</a>

5) Simon Buckle wrote his second piece on Riak for IBM developerWorks. This
one is about using Riak as a cache.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.ibm.com/developerworks/opensource/library/os-riak2/index.html">http://www.ibm.com/developerworks/opensource/library/os-riak2/index.html</a>

6) This is a bit old but I&#x27;m including it because it&#x27;s worthwhile (and I
neglected to recap it when it was first published): Srdjan Pejic wrote a
post called &quot;Riak Search as a Means of Querying&quot; that you should read.

* Blog here ---&gt;
<a href="http://batasrki.github.com/blog/2012/04/24/riak-search-as-a-means-of-querying/">http://batasrki.github.com/blog/2012/04/24/riak-search-as-a-means-of-querying/</a>

7) Basho and Riak are going to have a big presence at Strange Loop in a few
months (more to come on that soon)... To start things off, Sean Cribbs&#x27;
&quot;Eventually Consistent Data Structures&quot; talk was accepted. (Pay special
attention to this if you care about stricter consistency guarantees on top
of Riak.) Also, go register for Strange Loop.

* Talk details here ---&gt;
<a href="https://thestrangeloop.com/sessions/eventually-consistent-data-structures">https://thestrangeloop.com/sessions/eventually-consistent-data-structures</a>

8) Boundary hacker Dietrich Featherston gave a talk last week on one of
their internal systems called Kobayashi and touches on how it makes use of
Riak. (Also, if you&#x27;re in/around San Francisco and aren&#x27;t signed up for the
Boundary Tech Talk Meetup Group, I highly recommend you do so:
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/Boundary-Tech-Talks/">http://www.meetup.com/Boundary-Tech-Talks/</a>)

* Slides from his talk here ---&gt;
<a href="http://dl.dropbox.com/u/1133314/kobayashi/large-scale-olap-with-kobayashi.pdf">http://dl.dropbox.com/u/1133314/kobayashi/large-scale-olap-with-kobayashi.pdf</a>

9) Steve Vinoski&#x27;s &quot;Objects, Anomalies, and Actors: The Next Revolution&quot;
talk from QConSF 2011 is now online.

* Watch here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Objects-Anomalies-and-Actors-The-Next-Revolution">http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Objects-Anomalies-and-Actors-The-Next-Revolution</a>

10) Bradley Taylor and his team at Rails Machine are looking for a Web
Operations Engineer. There&#x27;s a non-trivial chance you&#x27;ll get to do some
ridiculous things with Riak.

* Details here ---&gt;
<a href="https://jobs.github.com/positions/327aceca-a04f-11e1-8feb-fd5ed15f1fe8">https://jobs.github.com/positions/327aceca-a04f-11e1-8feb-fd5ed15f1fe8</a>

11) There are a few new pieces of Riak content up on SA.

* Best practices for MapReduce query on Secondary Indexes with AND
conditions and ordering with the Java Client ---&gt;
<a href="http://stackoverflow.com/questions/10662221/riak-java-best-practices-for-mapreduce-query-on-secondary-indexes-with-and-con">http://stackoverflow.com/questions/10662221/riak-java-best-practices-for-mapreduce-query-on-secondary-indexes-with-and-con</a>
* ORM lirbary for Riak on Node.js ---&gt;
<a href="http://stackoverflow.com/questions/10608521/orm-lirbary-for-riak-on-node-js">http://stackoverflow.com/questions/10608521/orm-lirbary-for-riak-on-node-js</a>
