bring to your attention. This repo is home of the Riak Community Release
Notes.  Here&#x27;s the first installment (spanning April 1 - May 3):

<a href="https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md">https://github.com/basho/the-riak-community/blob/master/release-notes/riak-community-0.2.md</a>
