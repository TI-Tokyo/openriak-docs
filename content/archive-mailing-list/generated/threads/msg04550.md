Riak Recap for August 29 - 31
======================

1) Haris Amin wrote a quick blog post about installing Riak on OS X
Lion. Thanks, Haris!

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://blog.harisamin.com/post/9658193785/install-riak-on-os-x-lion">http://blog.harisamin.com/post/9658193785/install-riak-on-os-x-lion</a>

2) The Chicago Boss Web Framework now supports Riak as a backend.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="https://twitter.com/#!/basho/status/108982455062634497">https://twitter.com/#!/basho/status/108982455062634497</a>

3) This is a ways off but still worth relaying: Jon Brisbin will be
giving a talk on Riak and evented data with RabbitMQ at the Springone
conference in Chicago at the end of October.

* Talk details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.springone2gx.com/conference/chicago/2011/10/speakers/jon_brisbin">http://www.springone2gx.com/conference/chicago/2011/10/speakers/jon_brisbin</a>

4) There will be a half day Riak training at the upcoming Surge
Conference in Baltimore happening at the end of September. The best
part about this is that it&#x27;s free with your registration (and Surge&#x27;s
lineup looks downright amazing).

* Come to this ---&gt; <a href="http://omniti.com/surge/2011/sessions/training#riak-training">http://omniti.com/surge/2011/sessions/training#riak-training</a>

5) Duncan McGreggor has a new repo up on Launchpad that connects Riak
to Django. Thanks, Duncan!

* Django Riak Engine ---&gt; <a href="https://launchpad.net/django-riak-engine">https://launchpad.net/django-riak-engine</a>
