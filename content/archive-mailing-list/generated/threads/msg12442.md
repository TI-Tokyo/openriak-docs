# San Diego

I&#x27;ll be in San Diego on October 10th with Basho hacker Brett Hazen to
make the San Diego Riak Meetup [1] official. We&#x27;ll be at the Sony

Matt Snyder is getting a Cleveland Riak Meetup [2] off the ground.

the &quot;Forest City,&quot; you should make an appearance.

to make up for it. :)
