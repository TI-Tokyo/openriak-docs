<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb_socket.erl#L454">https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb_socket.erl#L454</a>

<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb_socket.erl#L618">https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb_socket.erl#L618</a>

Cheers -
Dave
