<a href="https://www.nuget.org/packages/RiakClient/">https://www.nuget.org/packages/RiakClient/</a>

*<a href="http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/">http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/</a>
&lt;<a href="http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/">http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/</a>&gt;*

We would like to give special thanks to Jeremiah Peschka and OJ Reeves for
their hard work on CorrugatedIron, and for passing on CI&#x27;s stewardship to
Basho so we could continue its story.

Thanks!
