Riak Recap for May 1 - May 6
======================

John Daily has started a series of Basho blog posts on the subtleties
of Riak configuration.
- <a href="http://basho.com/understanding-riaks-configurable-behaviors-part-1/">http://basho.com/understanding-riaks-configurable-behaviors-part-1/</a>

John Liao wrote a blog post about Exploring Riak with F# and CorrugatedIron.
-
<a href="http://jyliao.blogspot.com/2013/04/exploring-riak-with-f-and-corrugatediron.html">http://jyliao.blogspot.com/2013/04/exploring-riak-with-f-and-corrugatediron.html</a>

riak-js v0.10.1 is out.
-
<a href="http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2013-May/012017.html">http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2013-May/012017.html</a>
- <a href="http://riak-js.org/">http://riak-js.org/</a>

the process of adding Protocol Buffers support to Node.js, and
eventually riak-js.
- <a href="http://blog.andyet.com/2013/may/7/riak-protocol-buffer-encoding-and-you/">http://blog.andyet.com/2013/may/7/riak-protocol-buffer-encoding-and-you/</a>
