If you are doing anything that will read from disk, SSDs will improve your
performance. If you are doing any reads from disk that are non-sequential,
SSDs will really increase your performance.

However, make sure you do the reading on the SSDs you&#x27;re going to purchase
because not all SSDs are created equal. I had a client buy some smaller
OCZ-Vertex 3s recently which have a wear leveling issue (the 120GB drives
have fewer chips than the 240GB drives) that causes performance to fall
apart pretty quickly.

In general, for production you want to use SLC or eMLC drives (which are
quite a bit more expensive than MLC but also have fewer degredation
issues).

Another way you can improve your life significantly I/O wise is to use
an OS with ZFS (such as SmartOS, IllumOS or Solaris) as these can be
configured to use the SSDs as a large cache for reads and write backs to
slower I/O such as SAS or SATA.
