The value for content-type should be a string.

For custom meta data the client expects a proplist of custom headers under
the &lt;&lt;&quot;X-Riak-Meta&quot;&gt;&gt; key. Check out the following test to get an idea of
what the metadata would look like:
<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb.erl#L311">https://github.com/basho/riak-erlang-client/blob/master/src/riakc_pb.erl#L311</a>

Thanks,

quickly. I think I&#x27;ve even asked this before and couldn&#x27;t figure out why what I
was doing wasn&#x27;t working.

I had to go through the list of custom properties with keys like (&quot;test&quot;) and
turn them into keys like &quot;X-Riak-Meta-test&quot; and stick those in a proplist
hanging off the &quot;X-Riak-Meta&quot; key.

Thanks for setting me straight. ;)
