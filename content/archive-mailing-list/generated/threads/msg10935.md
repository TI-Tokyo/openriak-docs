  Bitcask merging is normal, but combined with incoming handoff, it may be
overloading the node.
Two things you might try:
-reduce handoff_concurrency to 1 on all nodes to reduce the impact of
handoff (<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/references/Configuration-Files/">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/references/Configuration-Files/</a>)
-restrict when Bitcask is allowed to merge by setting the merge_window on
the node that is being overloaded
(<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/tutorials/choosing-a-backend/Bitcask/#Con">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/tutorials/choosing-a-backend/Bitcask/#Con</a>
figuring-Bitcask)
