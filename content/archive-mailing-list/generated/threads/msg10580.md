</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg10633" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Eric Redmond</span><time datetime="2013-03-31T14:39:09-07:00">March 31, 2013 at 2:39 PM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: costs of scaling riak compared to mangodb</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">One practical matter to also keep in mind is the number of nodes you must scale
to and by.

The recommended minimum nodes when running Riak in production is five servers.
Once you start with that minimum, scaling is a matter of adding single nodes as
you need them, six to seven to eight and so on.

Conversely, mongo scales by threes (one master and two secondary&#x27;s in a
replset). So you start with three, then six, then nine, etc.

Hope that helps,
