8)  Q --- Is there a reason why the default n value for riak is 3?
(via mheld from #riak)

     A --- It&#x27;s a sensible default - Lamport says you need at least 3
computers for fault-tolerance. It&#x27;s  also because 3 is the lowest
value at which quorum and all are not the same .
