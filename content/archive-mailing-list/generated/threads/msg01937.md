name. The bucket+key string is then run through SHA1. Don&#x27;t take that as
gospel, though. I searched my mailing list archive and couldn&#x27;t find it, but
this also could have happened on the IRC channel. Hopefully someone who
knows the source can point you in the right direction.

Until then, you can safely assume that the hashed key is the result of
SHA1(bucket_name + key_name). You should be able to determine if they&#x27;re on
the same node by hashing both bucket/key combos and then looking at the
ring. Take a look at
<a href="http://wiki.basho.com/Replication.html#Routing-an-object-to-a-set-of-partitionsfor">http://wiki.basho.com/Replication.html#Routing-an-object-to-a-set-of-partitionsfor</a>
more info on looking at the state of the ring.

</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg01940" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Sean Cribbs</span><time datetime="2011-01-10T05:15:27-08:00">January 10, 2011 at 5:15 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: Can I store the different bucket objects but with same key in the	same place</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">To clear up this confusion, let me spell it out. The hash value of any
bucket/key pair is the SHA1 of the serialized tuple of {Bucket, Key}. That is,
{Bucket, Key} -&gt; term_to_binary -&gt; sha1.

The actual function that does the hashing:

<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_core/blob/master/src/chash.erl#L140">https://github.com/basho/riak_core/blob/master/src/chash.erl#L140</a>

The helper function that most riak_core applications use:

<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_core/blob/master/src/riak_core_util.erl#L160">https://github.com/basho/riak_core/blob/master/src/riak_core_util.erl#L160</a>

An example of usage in the Get FSM:

<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_kv/blob/master/src/riak_kv_get_fsm.erl#L70">https://github.com/basho/riak_kv/blob/master/src/riak_kv_get_fsm.erl#L70</a>
