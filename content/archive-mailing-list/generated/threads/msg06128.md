Riak Recap for December 28 - January 3
==============================

1) A Riak script was added to Nmap

* Announcement here ---&gt; <a href="http://seclists.org/nmap-dev/2012/q1/11">http://seclists.org/nmap-dev/2012/q1/11</a>
* Script details here ---&gt; <a href="http://nmap.org/nsedoc/scripts/riak-http-info.html">http://nmap.org/nsedoc/scripts/riak-http-info.html</a>

2) The next Bay Area Riak Meetup is happening on January 18th at the
StackMob offices.

* Details and registration here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/46649472">http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/46649472</a>

3) John Leach wrote a great guide for running Riak on the BrightBox Cloud.

* Read and build here ---&gt; <a href="http://brightbox.com/blog/2012/01/04/riak-cluster/">http://brightbox.com/blog/2012/01/04/riak-cluster/</a>

4) For anyone who&#x27;ll be in/around Copenhagen in early March: Basho,
Trifork, and Erlang Solutions will be holding another NoSQL Roadshow
on March 2nd.

* Details here ---&gt;  <a href="http://nosqlroadshow.com/nosql-cph-2012/">http://nosqlroadshow.com/nosql-cph-2012/</a>

5) This isn&#x27;t specific to Riak but very applicable and interesting
nonetheless: a group at Berkeley is working on testing just how
&quot;eventual&quot; eventual consistency actually is in Dynamo-style systems.
You might be surprised...

* Research and in-browser demo here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pbailis/projects/pbs/#">http://www.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pbailis/projects/pbs/#</a>

6) 0.2.3 of ldapjs-riak is now available.

* On GitHub here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/mcavage/node-ldapjs-riak">https://github.com/mcavage/node-ldapjs-riak</a>

7) The Riak Knowledge Base got a lot of updates over the past week.
(For those of you who don&#x27;t yet know about the KB, enjoy.)
