* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/19806">http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/19806</a>

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/19762">http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/19762</a>

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/18959">http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011/public/schedule/detail/18959</a>

In addition to Rusty (@rustyio), Basho will also be officially
represented at OSCON by Tony Falco (@antonyfalco), Ryan Zezeski
