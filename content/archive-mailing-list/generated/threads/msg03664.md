Riak Recap for June 10 - 12
=====================

1) Slide decks from last week&#x27;s Erlang Factory London Conference are
already starting to appear (thanks, Erlang Solutions!). Here are the
first few that involve Basho and Riak:

* Jon Brisin&#x27;s &quot;Eventing Data with RabbitMQ and Riak&quot; ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/429/EventingDatawithRabbitMQandRiak.pdf">http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/429/EventingDatawithRabbitMQandRiak.pdf</a>
* Kresten Krab Thorup&#x27;s &quot;Bring Riak to the Mobile Platform&quot; ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/413/ErlangFactory2011_KrestenKrabThorup_RiakSync.pdf">http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/413/ErlangFactory2011_KrestenKrabThorup_RiakSync.pdf</a>
* Scott Lystig Fritchie&#x27;s &quot;Usually Received, Maybe Late, or Sometimes
Dropped&quot; ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/403/SLF.protocol-verification.pdf">http://www.erlang-factory.com/upload/presentations/403/SLF.protocol-verification.pdf</a>

2) Mathias Meyer posted his slides from NodeConfEU that happened this
past weekend.

* Peruse here --&gt; <a href="http://riaktant-nodecamp.heroku.com/#1">http://riaktant-nodecamp.heroku.com/#1</a>

3) Charlie Robbins and the team at Nodejitsu are doing some work to
refactor the Riak support in Winston, their &quot;multi-transport async
logging library for node.js.&quot;

* Latest progress is here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/indexzero/winston-riak">https://github.com/indexzero/winston-riak</a>

4) One new job to pass along to you:

* Styles Club is hiring a &quot;Riak (and Ripple) Expert&quot; ---&gt;
<a href="http://jobs.nosqlweekly.com/jobs/riak-and-ripple-expert-wanted/">http://jobs.nosqlweekly.com/jobs/riak-and-ripple-expert-wanted/</a>

5) Andy Gross will be on the &quot;Big Data, No SQL, Big Problems, No
Worries&quot; panel happening tomorrow at the HotStorage.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.usenix.org/events/hotstorage11/tech/">http://www.usenix.org/events/hotstorage11/tech/</a>
