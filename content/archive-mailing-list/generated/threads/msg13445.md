</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg13448" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Jon Brisbin</span><time datetime="2014-01-22T06:08:10-08:00">January 22, 2014 at 6:08 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: Why should i choose riak over redis?</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">I look at it like this: if you can fit your entire database into memory and
don&#x27;t mind losing the entire thing when the server goes down or becomes
unavailable and you like the extra functionality of Redis like hashes,
counters, and messaging, then go with Redis. If you can&#x27;t afford to lose the
data you put into it and you need the availability of more than a single server
and don&#x27;t use the simple messaging of Redis, then go with Riak.

That said, the two are not direct competitors. They may have overlapping
functionality in some areas but they each address fundamentally different
concerns.
