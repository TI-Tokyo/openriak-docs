</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg15484" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Charlie Voiselle</span><time datetime="2015-01-13T09:15:44-08:00">January 13, 2015 at 9:15 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: How to find which nodes host a particular bucket/key?</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">You can use the following Erlang snippet in `riak attach`:

N=3,
{ok,Ring} = riak_core_ring_manager:get_my_ring(),
DocIdx = riak_core_util:chash_key({&lt;&lt;&quot;my_bucket&quot;&gt;&gt;, &lt;&lt;&quot;my_key&quot;&gt;&gt;}),
&lt;&lt;I:160/integer&gt;&gt; = DocIdx,
Preflist = riak_core_ring:preflist(DocIdx, Ring),
{Targets, Fallbacks} = lists:split(N, Preflist),
Targets.

Verify that `N` matches your clusters N-val. Change `my_bucket` and `my_key` as
appropriate.  If all of your nodes are up and healthy, this will give you the
primaries in the `Targets` variable and the fallback partitions in order of
preference in `Fallbacks`.
