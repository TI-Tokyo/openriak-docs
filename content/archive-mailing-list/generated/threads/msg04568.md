Riak Recap for September 1 - 2
=======================

1) Basho Developer Ryan Zezeski is in Baltimore and is looking for
other like-minded Baltimoreans for talk Riak with (for meetups.
presos, etc.). If this is you, shoot an email to rzeze...@basho.com.

* Some details here ---&gt;
<a href="https://twitter.com/#!/rzezeski/status/109123324851929088">https://twitter.com/#!/rzezeski/status/109123324851929088</a>

<a href="http://blog.basho.com/2011/09/02/John-Newman-Has-Joined-The-Basho-Team/">http://blog.basho.com/2011/09/02/John-Newman-Has-Joined-The-Basho-Team/</a>
