According to the error &#x27;error_verifying_props&#x27;, it looks like your
Riak KV couldn&#x27;t be configured for Riak CS. Please make sure if Riak
KV is configured as to allow_mult[1] and backend[2].

[1]:
<a href="https://docs.basho.com/riak/cs/2.1.1/cookbooks/configuration/riak-for-cs/#allowing-for-sibling-creation">https://docs.basho.com/riak/cs/2.1.1/cookbooks/configuration/riak-for-cs/#allowing-for-sibling-creation</a>
[2]:

Thanks,
Kaz
