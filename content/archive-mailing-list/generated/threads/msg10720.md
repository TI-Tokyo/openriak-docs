</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg10721" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Alexander Moore</span><time datetime="2013-04-05T13:53:11-07:00">April 5, 2013 at 1:53 PM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: WARNING: All of &#x27;riak@10.1.42.11&#x27; replicas will be lost</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">Hey Elias,

It&#x27;s referring to the vnode replicas on the node you are replacing.  Doing
a force-replace reassigns all that nodes partitions without handing them
off, so the replicas on that node are &quot;lost&quot;.  However, since riak
replicates to &#x27;N&#x27; physical nodes, there should be other replicas of your
data.
It will take a repair to replicate the data to the new node though.

Thanks,
Alex
