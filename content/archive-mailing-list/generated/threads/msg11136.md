curl -v -s -o/dev/null -H &quot;Accept: image/*&quot;
<a href="http://xxxxxxx:8098/riak/45/21601_7803328_3.jpg">http://xxxxxxx:8098/riak/45/21601_7803328_3.jpg</a> --&gt; Works

my question though -- is the same key 21601_7803328_3 associated with
different values of different content types? We set in our config:

{allow_mult,false},
{last_write_wins,true},

I&#x27;d imagine one would overwrite the other, depending on which one comes in
late?
