redundancy guarantees are compromised in a production environment.

So initially I was simply publishing the required ports to the real host
interface, and then doing clustering as you would normally between Riak
hosts on a network.

Now I am implementing Rudder (<a href="https://coreos.com/blog/introducing-rudder/">https://coreos.com/blog/introducing-rudder/</a>),
which is a much better solution.
