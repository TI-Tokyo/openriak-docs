*State of the Union*

Since we haven&#x27;t had one of these in a while, here is an update on the

*Announcements*

   - I (Matt) have joined on as a Developer Advocate. If you&#x27;re wondering
   who I am, you can check out this blog post
   &lt;<a href="http://neckbeardinfluence.com/pursuing-my-inner-neckbeard-headed-to-basho/">http://neckbeardinfluence.com/pursuing-my-inner-neckbeard-headed-to-basho/</a>&gt;
   or ping me on Twitter &lt;<a href="https://twitter.com/mjbrender">https://twitter.com/mjbrender</a>&gt;

   IRC &amp; email. More to come on that
