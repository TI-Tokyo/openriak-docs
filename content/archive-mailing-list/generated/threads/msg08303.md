Riak Recap for August 4 - 15
=====================

1) We added a whole mess of RICON talks to the schedule (and we still
have a few more to announce). This is a reminder that you should
attend RICON and ping me for a discount code.

* Talk descriptions here ---&gt; <a href="http://lanyrd.com/2012/ricon2012/">http://lanyrd.com/2012/ricon2012/</a>
* Conference site here ---&gt; ricon2012.com
* Buy tickets for you and your friends here ---&gt;  ricon2012.eventbrite.com

2) We posted the video of the Riak at Braintree talk that happened
last month at the Chicago meetup. This one is well worth your 30
minutes.

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/08/14/riak-at-braintree/">http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/08/14/riak-at-braintree/</a>

3) The Riak Homebrew Recipe was updated for 1.2.

* Details here ---&gt;
<a href="https://twitter.com/HomebrewUpdates/status/232898132076285952">https://twitter.com/HomebrewUpdates/status/232898132076285952</a>

4) Basho hacker Ryan Zezeski has some experimental with Riak and Solr.
(It&#x27;s worth noting that this code is inspired by work that John
Muellerleile started some months ago. )

* Code here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/rzezeski/yokozuna">https://github.com/rzezeski/yokozuna</a>

5) The H-Online wrote a piece about 1.2&#x27;s official FreeBSD support.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.h-online.com/open/news/item/Riak-clustered-NoSQL-database-adds-official-FreeBSD-support-1662846.html">http://www.h-online.com/open/news/item/Riak-clustered-NoSQL-database-adds-official-FreeBSD-support-1662846.html</a>

be interesting to any of you Cloud Foundry fans out there.)

* Code here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/BrianMMcClain/riak-release">https://github.com/BrianMMcClain/riak-release</a>

will be sharing details on their live Mongo to Riak migration. It&#x27;s

* Details here --&gt; <a href="http://www.meetup.com/Boston-Riak/events/77828202/">http://www.meetup.com/Boston-Riak/events/77828202/</a>

* Code here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_nagios">https://github.com/basho/riak_nagios</a>

eventual consistency, by odbms.org.

11) TransactCharlie posted a video of a lightning talk called &quot;The CAP

speaker.)

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://vimeo.com/47379379">http://vimeo.com/47379379</a>

12) A video of Kresten Krab Thorup&#x27;s &quot;Bringing Riak to the Mobile
Platform&quot; talk from QCon London is now online and viewable.

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Riak-BucketDB-Mobile">http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Riak-BucketDB-Mobile</a>

I am going to go out on a limb and suggest that Basho&#x27;s scripts are
going to be &quot;nicer&quot; to your Riak nodes. We&#x27;ve been polling the stats
page on our Riak nodes at the rate of about once-every-10-seconds for
almost a year now with no visible performance problems, so I&#x27;m
comfortable saying that the stats JSON doesn&#x27;t cause any real
problems.

That said, use whatever fits your needs. Basho&#x27;s scripts don&#x27;t (yet?)
let you alert based on latencies, sibling counts, object sizes, or how
many nodes you&#x27;ve lost from your cluster. If they complete that
functionality, I&#x27;d be happy to switch over to using the official
scripts. :)
