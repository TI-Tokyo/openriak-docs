5)  Q --- On the Links and Link Walking page it says Riak can
substitute for a graph DB &quot;as long as the number of links is kept
reasonably low.&quot; What &quot;number of links&quot; does this refer to? The total

links on any given document? (from pjstadig via #riak)

     A -- The number of links on any given document. (I&#x27;ve updated the
wiki to clarify that.)
