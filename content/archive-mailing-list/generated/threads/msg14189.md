You want to use the &#x27;wt=json&#x27; parameter, in your query (see the Solr
section of <a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/dev/using/search/">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/dev/using/search/</a> )
So, your request would be:
<a href="http://127.0.0.1:10018/solr/index_bucket/select?q=key:Value&amp;wt=json">http://127.0.0.1:10018/solr/index_bucket/select?q=key:Value&amp;wt=json</a>

(As a side note, in HTTP terminology, the &#x27;Content-Type: application/json&#x27;
header describes the format of your _request_, not the response).
