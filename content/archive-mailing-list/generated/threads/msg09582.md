You can&#x27;t just give it the filename, you need to read the file.  Something
like this should work:

to_pdf(ReqData, Context) -&gt;
  {ok, Body} = file:read_file(&quot;/root/sicap-rpt/reports/javascript.pdf&quot;),
  {Body, ReqData, Context}.
