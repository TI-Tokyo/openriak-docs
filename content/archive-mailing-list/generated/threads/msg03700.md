It is not safe to share a single Riak client instance across multiple
client-facing threads.

Riak&#x27;s conflict detection mechanisms will be misled by that sort of
sharing.  Luckily, the client is quite lightweight so you shouldn&#x27;t
have to worry about the cost of doing it right.
