This is likely the result of heavy load on Riak; how much have you tuned
Riak &amp; your OS?  Please make sure to review our tuning documentation.

Thought it&#x27;s not clear in our CS documentation, make sure you have the
leveldb backend properly tuned:
LevelDB backend:
<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/ops/advanced/backends/leveldb/">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/ops/advanced/backends/leveldb/</a>

You might want to try increasing the request_pool and bucket_list_pool
values in your cs app.config, making sure to keep the value of
pb_backlog in Riak greater than the sum of the two pool values.  The
recommended pb_backlog value is 256.

<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/configuration/Configuring-Riak/">http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/configuration/Configuring-Riak/</a>
<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/configuration/Configuring-Riak-CS/">http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/configuration/Configuring-Riak-CS/</a>

If you go to <a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/</a> you can find an entire
section on Tuning under &quot;Riak for Operators&quot; in the left navigation that
might help you tune your cluster.
