<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak/blob/1.4/RE">https://github.com/basho/riak/blob/1.4/RE</a> LEASE-NOTES.md

<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak.git">https://github.com/basho/riak.git</a>

Thanks,
