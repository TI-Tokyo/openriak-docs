We are pleased to announce the availability of Riak 1.4.2.   This is a
bugfix release based on the 1.4.x series of Riak.

The major subsystems with fixes are:

   - Fixed various problems related to crashing stats
   - Fixed extra noisy logs introduced in 1.4.1 (Not found errors and
   others)
   - Fixed issues related to 2i queries timing out
   - Added more protection against corrupt data in backends
   - Fixed incorrect capability negotiation causing nodes to appear
   incompatible in Riak Control

The full release notes can be found here:
<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak/blob/1.4/RELEASE-NOTES.md">https://github.com/basho/riak/blob/1.4/RELEASE-NOTES.md</a>

Finally, the packages can be found on our docs page here:
<a href="http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/downloads/">http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/downloads/</a>

Thanks to everyone who reported bugs and were extremely patient while
waiting for these fixes. Also, thanks again to everyone in the Riak
community for using our software!

-Everyone at Basho
