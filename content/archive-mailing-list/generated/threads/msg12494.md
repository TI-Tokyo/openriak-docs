</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg12506" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Sean Cribbs</span><time datetime="2013-10-03T10:53:35-07:00">October 3, 2013 at 10:53 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: Riak ring size</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">Your main concern with greater numbers of partitions is contention for IO
(and CPU time for the vnodes to process requests). Consider that with 6
nodes, you will have 170(+/- 1) partitions running on each physical node.
That&#x27;s *at least* 170 files open, all scribbling to and reading from disk.
 You might get away with 512, but my experience tells me to be wary of that
for small clusters. I think unless you&#x27;re doing big-web-property-level
traffic you won&#x27;t need to grow your cluster as large as you think,
especially with machines that beefy.
