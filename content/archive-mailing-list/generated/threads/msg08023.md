Your index field must have a &quot;_bin&quot; or &quot;_int&quot; suffix on it to signify
whether it is a binary or an integer. Add &quot;_bin&quot; to the field name you have
in your example, e.g.

MetaData = dict:store(&lt;&lt;&quot;index&quot;&gt;&gt;, [{&lt;&lt;&quot;afield_bin&quot;&gt;&gt;, &lt;&lt;&quot;avalue&quot;&gt;&gt;}],
dict:new())
