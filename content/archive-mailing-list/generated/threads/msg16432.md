The fastest way would be to chunk your file, and use a Parallel.ForEach
loop to do parallel puts.  Just make sure you have your connection pool size
&lt;<a href="http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/html/P_RiakClient_Config_RiakNodeConfiguration_PoolSize.htm">http://basho.github.io/riak-dotnet-client-api/html/P_RiakClient_Config_RiakNodeConfiguration_PoolSize.htm</a>&gt;
turned up, and you override the default MaxDegreesOfParallelism
&lt;<a href="https://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/library/system.threading.tasks.paralleloptions.maxdegreeofparallelism(v=vs.110).aspx">https://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/library/system.threading.tasks.paralleloptions.maxdegreeofparallelism(v=vs.110).aspx</a>&gt;
limit
too (these two should match, or the pool should be bigger).

Thanks,
Alex
