</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg07760" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Erik Søe Sørensen</span><time datetime="2012-06-24T03:12:17-07:00">June 24, 2012 at 3:12 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">RE: JSON-Encoding from riakc</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">One answer is: dict:to_list/1 and dict:from_list may be your friends.
Another is: Are you sure that dict is appropriate for your data? If the number
of entries is bounded and does not exceed around a dozen, then a property list
might be faster.
(Although I&#x27;d advice you to use lists:keyfind(Key, 1, PropList) rather than
proplist:get_value(); there&#x27;s a significant speed difference.)
Thirdly, you can of course translate to JSON and back, but keeping the data on
Erlang term form is probably both simpler and faster.

/Erik
