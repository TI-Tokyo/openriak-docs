These errors are almost certainly harmless and being thrown when empty,
non-owned vnodes get shut down.

It appears that in some cases, the underlying ets table might already be
deleted/GC&#x27;d by the time BackendModule:stop tries to explicitly delete it.
 I&#x27;ve opened this bug to track the issue:
<a href="http://issues.basho.com/show_bug.cgi?id=723">http://issues.basho.com/show_bug.cgi?id=723</a>
