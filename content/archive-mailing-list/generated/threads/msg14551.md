You can change the number of allowed transfers via the riak-admin tool:

$ riak-admin transfer-limit
=============================== Transfer Limit
================================
Limit        Node
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    2        &#x27;riak@127.0.0.1&#x27;
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Note: You can change transfer limits with &#x27;riak-admin transfer_limit
&lt;limit&gt;&#x27;
      and &#x27;riak-admin transfer_limit &lt;node&gt; &lt;limit&gt;&#x27;
