Riak Recap for November 16 - 17
========================

1) Greg Stein continues to do great work on the Riak Python Client.
(Thanks, Greg!)

* His latest contributions are here ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-python-client/commits/master">https://github.com/basho/riak-python-client/commits/master</a>
