Riak Recap for March 7 - 8

1) Franck Cuny and the team at Linkfluence published an extensive (and
downright fantastic) blog post about moving from CouchDB to Riak that
appeared early Monday.

*Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://labs.linkfluence.net/nosql/2011/03/07/moving_from_couchdb_to_riak.html">http://labs.linkfluence.net/nosql/2011/03/07/moving_from_couchdb_to_riak.html</a>

Thanks, Franck!

1.1.) Linkfluence is also hiring. Jobs page here ---&gt;
<a href="http://labs.linkfluence.net/jobs.html">http://labs.linkfluence.net/jobs.html</a>

1.2) ReadWriteWeb picked up the Linkfluence blog post and published
their thoughts on it.

* Read here ---&gt; <a href="http://rww.to/gy9WU4">http://rww.to/gy9WU4</a>

2) Sean Cribbs wrote a blog post about Ripple and its use of Fibers.

* Blog here ---&gt; <a href="http://seancribbs.com/tech/2011/03/08/how-ripple-uses-fibers/">http://seancribbs.com/tech/2011/03/08/how-ripple-uses-fibers/</a>

3) There&#x27;s a Riak Search package in the Arch User Repository courtesy
of bougyman.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://aur.archlinux.org/packages.php?ID=47217">http://aur.archlinux.org/packages.php?ID=47217</a>

4)  We announced a Riak and Node.js webinar. This will be happening on
March 15th.

* Details and registration here ---&gt;
<a href="http://blog.basho.com/2011/03/08/free-webinar-riak-with-node/">http://blog.basho.com/2011/03/08/free-webinar-riak-with-node/</a>

5) The Python Client was bumped to version 1.2.1 and was pushed to PyPI.

* Package here ---&gt; <a href="http://pypi.python.org/pypi/riak/1.2.1">http://pypi.python.org/pypi/riak/1.2.1</a>

6) Jason J. Williams wrote a great post on his blog comparing Riak and
CouchDB for storing and querying coupon data based on their needs over
at DigiTar.

* Post here ---&gt;
<a href="http://blogs.digitar.com/jjww/2011/03/riak-vs-couchdb-for-storing-100000-coupons/">http://blogs.digitar.com/jjww/2011/03/riak-vs-couchdb-for-storing-100000-coupons/</a>

7) We announced the date and lineup for the next San Francisco Riak
Meetup. It&#x27;s happening on 3/22, and Coda Hale and Ryan Kennedy will be
talking about their experiences with Riak and Scala at Yammer.

* All the details and registration info here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/16833202/">http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/16833202/</a>

8) Daniel Einspanjer recorded a screenscast to share some of the
updates that he as made to the MapReduce front end that he uses with
the Mozilla Test Pilot project. This is a great tool for getting a
more visual handle on your MapReduce jobs and, as he notes in the
video, he&#x27;ll happily accept a pull request from anyone who is
interested in contributing.

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://screencast.com/t/KIzP2n7gx88">http://screencast.com/t/KIzP2n7gx88</a>

* Source code here ---&gt;
<a href="http://hg.mozilla.org/users/ibicking_mozilla.com/test-pilot-riak-frontend">http://hg.mozilla.org/users/ibicking_mozilla.com/test-pilot-riak-frontend</a>

9) We added PikkoTekk to the &quot;Who is Using Riak&quot; page on the wiki (h/t
to David Almroth for putting together the write up).

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://wiki.basho.com/Who-is-Using-Riak.html#pikkotekk">http://wiki.basho.com/Who-is-Using-Riak.html#pikkotekk</a>

Thanks for sharing, David!
