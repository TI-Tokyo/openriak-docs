Riak Recap for February 13 - 14
=======================

1) Balasundaram wrote a short blog post about building a multi-node Riak
cluster.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://balabtech.wordpress.com/2012/02/10/installation-and-config-of-multi-node-riak-cluster/">http://balabtech.wordpress.com/2012/02/10/installation-and-config-of-multi-node-riak-cluster/</a>

2) The video and slides from Sean Cribbs&#x27; Riak talk at the Gilt Group NoSQL
event are now online.

* Read and watch here ---&gt;
<a href="http://tech.gilt.com/post/17379979309/nosql-in-the-real-world-the-video-pics-and-slides">http://tech.gilt.com/post/17379979309/nosql-in-the-real-world-the-video-pics-and-slides</a>

3) A Riak extension for New Relic just appeared.

* Code here ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/newrelic/rpm_contrib/blob/master/lib/rpm_contrib/instrumentation/riak_client.rb">https://github.com/newrelic/rpm_contrib/blob/master/lib/rpm_contrib/instrumentation/riak_client.rb</a>

4) Steve Vinoski posted a write up on his blog with some details on the
track he&#x27;s hosting at QCon London in March.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://steve.vinoski.net/blog/2012/02/14/qcon-london-highly-available-systems/">http://steve.vinoski.net/blog/2012/02/14/qcon-london-highly-available-systems/</a>

5) OJ Reeves picked up his great series on Riak and Erlang and published
another extensive blog post. Readers of his past posts won&#x27;t
be disappointed with the fourth installment, and if you&#x27;re at all
interested in learning more about Riak, Webmachine, and/or ErlyDTL I highly
recommend you check these out.

* Latest post here ---&gt;
<a href="http://buffered.io/posts/webmachine-erlydtl-and-riak-part-4/">http://buffered.io/posts/webmachine-erlydtl-and-riak-part-4/</a>
