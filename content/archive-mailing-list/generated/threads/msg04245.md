Riak Recap for August 3 - 4
====================

1) Version 0.0.3 of django-riak-sessions just hit PyPI.

* Code here ---&gt;  <a href="http://pypi.python.org/pypi/django-riak-sessions/0.0.3">http://pypi.python.org/pypi/django-riak-sessions/0.0.3</a>
* On GitHub here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/flashingpumpkin/django-riak-sessions">https://github.com/flashingpumpkin/django-riak-sessions</a>

2)  Damon Oehlman wrote a blog post called &quot;Masterless Replication in
NoSQL Solutions&quot; and was looking for some people using Riak in
production to bounce some questions off of.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.distractable.net/coding/nosql-solutions-masterless-replication">http://www.distractable.net/coding/nosql-solutions-masterless-replication</a>

3) Nathan Marz (@nathanmarz) wrote a post for the Twitter Engineering
Blog all about Storm, the realtime computation system he and his team
built. Riak gets a few shout outs.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://engineering.twitter.com/2011/08/storm-is-coming-more-details-and-plans.html">http://engineering.twitter.com/2011/08/storm-is-coming-more-details-and-plans.html</a>

4) A video of Basho CTO Justin Sheehy and Damian Katz talking about
Riak, CouchDB, Erlang, and all sorts of other good stuff just
surfaced.

* Watch here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.infoq.com/interviews/sheehy-katz-riak-couchdb">http://www.infoq.com/interviews/sheehy-katz-riak-couchdb</a>

5) Bryan O&#x27;Sullivan&#x27;s &quot;Building Solid Distributed Applications with
Haskell and Riak&quot; from Erlang Factory is online and viewable.

* Watch here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Distributed-Applications-with-Haskell-and-Riak">http://www.infoq.com/presentations/Distributed-Applications-with-Haskell-and-Riak</a>

6) For anyone heading to the NoSQLNow Conference in San Jose in a few
weeks, you can expect to see Andy Gross on the &quot;Innovation in NoSQL
Data Stores&quot; panel.

* Details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://nosql2011.wilshireconferences.com/sessionPop.cfm?confid=64&amp;proposalid=4098">http://nosql2011.wilshireconferences.com/sessionPop.cfm?confid=64&amp;proposalid=4098</a>

7) Michael Klishin contributed a small fix to the Wiki.

* Pull request here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/basho/riak_wiki/pull/142">https://github.com/basho/riak_wiki/pull/142</a>

Thanks, Michael!

8) @NirvanaCore wrote an extensive comment on a Hacker News thread
with details on a new platform they are building. The one liner:
&quot;CoffeeScript based web apps on an erlang platform backed by Riak.&quot;
Keep an eye on them.

* Here&#x27;s the write-up ---&gt;<a href="http://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=2848647">http://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=2848647</a>
