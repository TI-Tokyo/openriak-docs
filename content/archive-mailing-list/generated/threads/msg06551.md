Riak Recap for February 3 - 5
======================

1) Basho Hacker Sean Cribbs will be talking about Riak at the &quot;NoSQL in The
Real World&quot; event that Gilt Group is putting on Wednesday night in New York
City.

* Event details here ---&gt; <a href="http://nosql-tech-talk.eventbrite.com/">http://nosql-tech-talk.eventbrite.com/</a>

from the forthcoming &quot;7 Databases in 7 Weeks&quot; book.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://buddylindsey.com/riak-week-2-of-7-databases-in-7-weeks">http://buddylindsey.com/riak-week-2-of-7-databases-in-7-weeks</a>

* Details and Registration here ---&gt;
<a href="http://skillsmatter.com/event/nosql/progressive-nosql-tutorials/ac-3538">http://skillsmatter.com/event/nosql/progressive-nosql-tutorials/ac-3538</a>
