Riak Recap for April 24 - 27
=====================

1) Today&#x27;s Recap is dedicated to John Leach. Here&#x27;s why:

* He posted his slides from the Riak Leeds meetup that happened last week
---&gt; <a href="http://johnleach.co.uk/downloads/slides/riak-consistent-hashing.pdf">http://johnleach.co.uk/downloads/slides/riak-consistent-hashing.pdf</a>
* He wrote a blog post about how Brightbox is using Riak Search ---&gt;
johnleach.co.uk/words/1063/riak-syslog
* He also released riak-syslog which lets you send your syslog messages
into Riak for searching in Riak Search ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/johnl/riak-syslog">https://github.com/johnl/riak-syslog</a>

2) Manuel Rubio wrote his second blog post about Riak, this one a follow up
to his initial overview.

* Read (in Spanish) here ---&gt;
<a href="http://bosqueviejo.net/2012/04/24/riak-revisando-y-practicando/">http://bosqueviejo.net/2012/04/24/riak-revisando-y-practicando/</a>

3) Pavlo Baron released a slide deck in which Riak features prominently.

* Check it out here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.slideshare.net/pavlobaron/from-hand-to-mouth-pavlobaron">http://www.slideshare.net/pavlobaron/from-hand-to-mouth-pavlobaron</a>

4) Pavlo also wrote a blog post about the riak_mongo code he and Kresten
have been writing.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://archi-jab-ture.blogspot.de/2012/04/riakmongo-making-riak-act-like-mongodb.html">http://archi-jab-ture.blogspot.de/2012/04/riakmongo-making-riak-act-like-mongodb.html</a>

5) Boundary wrote another blog post about Kobayashi, their distributed
analytics database that makes use of Riak.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://blog.boundary.com/2012/04/25/hungry-kobayashi-part-2/">http://blog.boundary.com/2012/04/25/hungry-kobayashi-part-2/</a>

6) The team at Prezi released a slide deck with some details on how and why
they are using Riak.

* Slides here ---&gt; <a href="http://prezi.com/qpc9flfkdvzz/riak-prezi/">http://prezi.com/qpc9flfkdvzz/riak-prezi/</a>

7) Sean Cribbs added a ton of information about Secondary Indexes to the
Ruby Client Wiki.

* Fall in love with documentation again here ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-ruby-client/wiki/Secondary-Indexes">https://github.com/basho/riak-ruby-client/wiki/Secondary-Indexes</a>

8) Shanley Kane wrote a piece for the Basho Blog about why your production
Riak clusters should have at least five nodes.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/04/27/Why-Your-Riak-Cluster-Should-Have-At-Least-Five-Nodes/">http://basho.com/blog/technical/2012/04/27/Why-Your-Riak-Cluster-Should-Have-At-Least-Five-Nodes/</a>

9) The team at Three Pillar Global wrote the first of three blog posts all
about Riak&#x27;s MapReduce

* Read here ---&gt; <a href="http://www.threepillarglobal.com/node/323">http://www.threepillarglobal.com/node/323</a>

10) Karl Seguin wrote blog post about Riak&#x27;s documentation and where he
thinks we should take it.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://openmymind.net/2012/4/28/How-Id-Change-Riaks-Documentation/">http://openmymind.net/2012/4/28/How-Id-Change-Riaks-Documentation/</a>
