1) For anyone on the Eastern Seaboard in the U.S., various basho
developers will be talking at three different conferences over the
next few days in Baltimore, Maryland.

You can get all the details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://localhost:8080/2010/09/29/basho-is-taking-over-baltimore-this-weekend/">http://localhost:8080/2010/09/29/basho-is-taking-over-baltimore-this-weekend/</a>

2) siculars, frank06, a fake frank06, ericlfo and jdmaturen had a
lengthy conversation about a specific use case and whether or not Riak
was the right fit.

Gist here ---&gt; <a href="http://gist.github.com/603324">http://gist.github.com/603324</a>

replicas, and what that means for the actual size of your data set.

Gist here ---&gt; <a href="http://gist.github.com/603334">http://gist.github.com/603334</a>

replication, CouchDB Cassandra, links, post-commit hooks and patches.

Gist here ---&gt; <a href="http://gist.github.com/603345">http://gist.github.com/603345</a>

Also, more on links ---&gt; <a href="http://wiki.basho.com/display/RIAK/Links">http://wiki.basho.com/display/RIAK/Links</a>,
<a href="http://wiki.basho.com/display/RIAK/Links+and+Link+Walking">http://wiki.basho.com/display/RIAK/Links+and+Link+Walking</a>

in Palo Alto at the end of October. A bunch of us from Basho will be
in attendance, and @argv0 will be taking part in the discussion.

More details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/Silicon-Valley-NoSQL/calendar/14727419/">http://www.meetup.com/Silicon-Valley-NoSQL/calendar/14727419/</a>
