I’m running Riak 2.0.2 on Centos 6.5 installed from an RPM
(<a href="http://s3.amazonaws.com/downloads.basho.com/riak/2.0/2.0.2/rhel/6/riak-2.0.2-1.el6.x86_64.rpm">http://s3.amazonaws.com/downloads.basho.com/riak/2.0/2.0.2/rhel/6/riak-2.0.2-1.el6.x86_64.rpm</a>)

I’m interested in wiping the entire storage (in order to re-run tests, etc). I
have a script that traverses buckets/keys but I’m aware that this process is
costly, plus I might be dealing with some eventual-consistency issues.

Consequently, I would prefer to wipe the “data” directory entirely if that’s in
any way possible, and re-start Riak. However, the structure seems to be
scattered in several directories and is not entirely clear which one needs to
be wiped for a “clean data reset” without corrupting anything:

# ll /var/lib/riak/

drwxrwxr-x.  2 riak riak 4096 Jan  4 07:23 riak_kv_exchange_fsm
drwxr-xr-x.  2 riak riak 4096 Jan  3 20:07 ring

</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg15437" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Sargun Dhillon</span><time datetime="2015-01-04T04:52:13-08:00">January 4, 2015 at 4:52 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: Wiping Riak 2.0 data</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">Assuming your storage engine is bitcask:
1. Turn off all your Riak nodes
2. rm -rf --verbose /var/lib/riak/bitcask/*/*
3. rm -rf --verbose /var/lib/riak/anti_entropy/*/*
4. Turn Riak back on

This will preserve your buckets and bucket types in in cluster
metadata. You can also automate that, but it&#x27;s a little more
complicated.

<blockquote>Assuming your storage engine is bitcask:

2. rm -rf --verbose /var/lib/riak/bitcask/*/*
3. rm -rf --verbose /var/lib/riak/anti_entropy/*/*
4. Turn Riak back on

This will preserve your buckets and bucket types in in cluster
metadata. You can also automate that, but it&#x27;s a little more
complicated.
