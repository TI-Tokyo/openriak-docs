However, I&#x27;d suggest the following: if they&#x27;re siblings, they were created from
concurrent edits. Thus, create a merge function that is entirely deterministic
without using the timestamp. This should save you from any clock skew issues,
and also from the fact that an edit may not have been performed with the most
up-to-date information.

I guess people can&#x27;t wait for our CRDTs to launch.
