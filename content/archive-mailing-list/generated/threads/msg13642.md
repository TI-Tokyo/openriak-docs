Riak Recap for January 24th - February 11th
===========================================

Riak CS 1.4.4 (bugfixes only) was released.
-
<a href="http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2014-February/014599.html">http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2014-February/014599.html</a>
- <a href="http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/Riak-CS-Release-Notes/">http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/cookbooks/Riak-CS-Release-Notes/</a>
- <a href="http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/riakcs-downloads/">http://docs.basho.com/riakcs/latest/riakcs-downloads/</a>

The team at GoGrid wrote a blog post that walks you through building a Riak
Cluster in 5 minutes.
-
<a href="http://blog.gogrid.com/2014/01/31/how-to-deploy-a-riak-cluster-in-5-minutes-on-gogrid/">http://blog.gogrid.com/2014/01/31/how-to-deploy-a-riak-cluster-in-5-minutes-on-gogrid/</a>

Seth Thomas hosted Riak Community Hangout #009, with Luc Perkins and Eric
Redmond discussing Basho&#x27;s documentation efforts.
- <a href="http://youtu.be/NH-n8JrwNJ8">http://youtu.be/NH-n8JrwNJ8</a>

Kiyota Tamura wrote a guest Basho blog post regarding Fluentd&#x27;s use of Riak.
- <a href="http://basho.com/fluentd-loves-riak/">http://basho.com/fluentd-loves-riak/</a>

Tom Santero and the Basho team tackled the eternal question: what are
advantages of Riak over Cassandra?
- <a href="http://basho.com/riak-vs-cassandra/">http://basho.com/riak-vs-cassandra/</a>

You too can participate in a Basho meme. Grab a databass t-shirt!
- <a href="http://teespring.com/databass2">http://teespring.com/databass2</a>

Tom Santero talked to NYLUG about Riak (wearing an older version of said
databass t-shirt).
- <a href="http://basho.com/basho-at-nylug/">http://basho.com/basho-at-nylug/</a>

Wherein Christopher Biscardi shares some of his first steps toward a Riak
Search client for Haskell.
-
<a href="http://www.christopherbiscardi.com/2014/02/01/in-search-of-a-riak-solr-client-for-haskell/">http://www.christopherbiscardi.com/2014/02/01/in-search-of-a-riak-solr-client-for-haskell/</a>
