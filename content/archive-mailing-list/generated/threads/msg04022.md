Riak Recap for July 13 - 14
====================

1) Myron Marston and the Ripple Committers have Ripple running on

Ruby community.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://twitter.com/#!/seancribbs/statuses/91579267246338048">http://twitter.com/#!/seancribbs/statuses/91579267246338048</a>
