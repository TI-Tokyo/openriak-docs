<a href="http://erldocs.com/R14B02/os_mon/memsup.html">http://erldocs.com/R14B02/os_mon/memsup.html</a>

There are also a series of stats prefixed with &quot;memory_&quot; which provide
memory information for the Riak node.
<a href="http://erldocs.com/R14B02/erts/erlang.html#memory/0">http://erldocs.com/R14B02/erts/erlang.html#memory/0</a>
