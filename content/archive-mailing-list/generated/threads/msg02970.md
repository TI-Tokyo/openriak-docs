Riak Recap for April 6 - 10
===================

1) A whole mess of Riak jobs appeared over the last few days:

* <a href="http://cb.com/etIeg9">http://cb.com/etIeg9</a>
* <a href="http://bit.ly/g05z35">http://bit.ly/g05z35</a>

* <a href="http://bit.ly/dRaDs5">http://bit.ly/dRaDs5</a>
* <a href="http://bit.ly/dSrxkt">http://bit.ly/dSrxkt</a>

2) Kyle Kingsbury (@aphyr) released a lightweight Ruby ORM for Riak
called Risky.

* On GitHub here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/aphyr/risky">https://github.com/aphyr/risky</a>

Thanks, Kyle!

3) Ripple 0.9.2, a minor bugfix release, is out. Definitely take a
look at these updates if you&#x27;re using Ripple.

* Details here ---&gt;  <a href="http://seancribbs.github.com/ripple/file.RELEASE_NOTES.html">http://seancribbs.github.com/ripple/file.RELEASE_NOTES.html</a>

4) Speaking of Ripple, Sean Cribbs added two new contributors to the project.

* Details here ---&gt; <a href="http://twitter.com/seancribbs/status/56418556115566593">http://twitter.com/seancribbs/status/56418556115566593</a>

Congrats to Kyle and Myron!

5) We announced a new webinar a few days ago. This one will take a
look at the Operational side of Riak and should be of particular
interest to anyone who foresees managing a Riak cluster in the near
future.

* Full details and registration here on the Basho blog ---&gt;
<a href="http://blog.basho.com/2011/04/07/free-webinar-riak-operations/">http://blog.basho.com/2011/04/07/free-webinar-riak-operations/</a>

6) The April Riak Meetup in San Francisco is on the calendar. This one
is happening on 4/27 and will feature Dan Reverri giving an extensive
preso on Riak Search.

* Info and RSVP details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/17212362/">http://www.meetup.com/San-Francisco-Riak-Meetup/events/17212362/</a>

(And, yes, we will be filming this talk.)

7) Another meetup reminder: Alexander Sicular and Francisco Treacy are
hosting a Riak Drinkup in NYC on 4/14.

* Read more here ---&gt;
<a href="http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2011-April/003788.html">http://lists.basho.com/pipermail/riak-users_lists.basho.com/2011-April/003788.html</a>

8) 0.4.0rc1 of riak-js was tagged a few days back.

* Code here ---&gt; <a href="https://github.com/frank06/riak-js">https://github.com/frank06/riak-js</a>

9) We added some Linux-specific info to the &quot;Open Files Limit&quot; page on
the wiki.

* Read up on it here ---&gt; <a href="http://wiki.basho.com/Open-Files-Limit.html">http://wiki.basho.com/Open-Files-Limit.html</a>
