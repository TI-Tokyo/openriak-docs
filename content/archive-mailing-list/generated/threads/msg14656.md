communications. E.g.

{ kernel, [
            {inet_dist_listen_min, 6000},
            {inet_dist_listen_max, 7999}
          ]},

Are there any recommendations on how many ports should be allowed. I&#x27;m assume

Can anyone provide guidance on this?
