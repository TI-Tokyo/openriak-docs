Riak Recap for September 12 - 13
=========================

1) Greg Stein and Reid Draper continue to do great stuff for the
Python client.

* Latest contributions here ---&gt;
<a href="https://github.com/basho/riak-python-client/commits/master">https://github.com/basho/riak-python-client/commits/master</a>

2) Kyle Kingsbury shared a sneak peak of the Riak metrics app he and
John Muellerleile will be showing off at the talk they are giving on
September 26th in San Francisco.

* Picture and talk details here ---&gt;
<a href="http://twitter.com/#!/aphyr/statuses/113444452374294528">http://twitter.com/#!/aphyr/statuses/113444452374294528</a>

HTTP responses that should be particularly interesting to any PHP devs
on the list.

* Read here ---&gt;
<a href="http://www.control-alt-del.org/2011/09/09/streaming-results-from-riak-over-http-in-php/">http://www.control-alt-del.org/2011/09/09/streaming-results-from-riak-over-http-in-php/</a>

* Read here ---&gt; <a href="http://blog.basho.com/2011/09/14/Secondary-Indexes-in-Riak/">http://blog.basho.com/2011/09/14/Secondary-Indexes-in-Riak/</a>
