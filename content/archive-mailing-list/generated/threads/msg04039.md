Riak Recap for July 18 - 19
====================

1) We announced a Riak and Python Webinar that&#x27;s happening on August
2nd and will be hosted by Basho Developer Advocate Mathias Meyer.

* Details and Registration here ---&gt;
<a href="http://blog.basho.com/2011/07/19/Riak-and-Python-Webinar-Happening-August-2/">http://blog.basho.com/2011/07/19/Riak-and-Python-Webinar-Happening-August-2/</a>
