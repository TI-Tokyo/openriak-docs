Deleting the ring and data directories after running &#x27;riak stop&#x27; will wipe the
node of all state and data. By default, the backend data and ring directories
are within the riak data directory(noted in app.config). Running and &quot;rm -rf
/path/to/data/dir&quot; once the node has been stopped will do the trick.

Let me know if you have questions.
