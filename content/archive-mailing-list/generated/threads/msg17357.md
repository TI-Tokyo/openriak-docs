</div><ol class="mailing-list-replies"><li class="mailing-list-reply"><details id="msg17358" class="mailing-list-message"><summary><header class="mailing-list-message__header"><span class="mailing-list-message__author">Vitaly</span><time datetime="2016-05-15T02:50:26-07:00">May 15, 2016 at 2:50 AM</time><span class="mailing-list-message__subject">Re: Riak search with case-insensitive searches</span></header></summary><div class="mailing-list-message__content"><div class="mailing-list-message__body">You can use a case-insensitive field type for this, for example

       &lt;fieldType name=&quot;string_ci&quot; class=&quot;solr.TextField&quot;
sortMissingLast=&quot;true&quot; omitNorms=&quot;true&quot;&gt;
           &lt;analyzer type=&quot;index&quot;&gt;
                &lt;tokenizer class=&quot;solr.KeywordTokenizerFactory&quot;/&gt;
                &lt;filter class=&quot;solr.LowerCaseFilterFactory&quot;/&gt;
            &lt;/analyzer&gt;
            &lt;analyzer type=&quot;query&quot;&gt;
                &lt;tokenizer class=&quot;solr.KeywordTokenizerFactory&quot;/&gt;
                &lt;filter class=&quot;solr.LowerCaseFilterFactory&quot;/&gt;
            &lt;/analyzer&gt;
        &lt;/fieldType&gt;

Of course, you&#x27;ll have to adjust your datatype/schema to make use of the
new type.

Regards,
Vitaly

match during search. Which means you won&#x27;t be able to use the same field
for case-sensitive search. Unfortunately, I&#x27;m not aware of other ways of
implementing case-insensitive search in Solr.
