Riak History

The Riak database, version 1.0, was originally released by Basho Technologies in 2011. Despite securing a niche within the database market, Basho technologies entered receivership in 2017. All assets were purchased from the receiver by a large Riak customer, bet365, who open-sourced all proprietary aspects .

Since that point, Riak has been maintained by pre-existing Riak users, and regular improvements have been made. In 2024 the OpenRiak community was formed with the intention of further progressing development of the Riak database, but also opening up the development process, with the aim of promoting adoption of Riak outside of its existing base of users.
